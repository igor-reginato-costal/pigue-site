Where we work

Greater São Paulo

Piguê manages the construction of houses and the renovation of houses and apartments in São Paulo, Osasco, Cotia, Barueri and Santana de Parnaíba.

In the city of São Paulo, we work in neighborhoods including Jardins and Vila São Francisco. In Cotia, our service area includes Granja Viana. In Barueri and Santana de Parnaíba, it includes Alphaville and Aldeia da Serra.

Inland and coastal São Paulo

For projects inland or on the coast of São Paulo state, we consider the location and scope individually.

Inland locations include Campinas, Sorocaba, Itu, Boituva, Porto Feliz and Itupeva. These include Fazenda Boa Vista and Boa Vista Estates in Porto Feliz, and Fazenda da Grama in Itupeva.
