The running track is located directly at the entrance for those arriving from the beach. In the study, the team would assess the exposure to saltwater, water drainage, and access for cleaning with the architect and the supplier. A sample of the joint between the floor and the window frame would allow for testing before installation.

The starting point

How the family wants to live

From the armchair to the shade, the sea is visible through the trees. With the glass doors open, the veranda becomes an extension of the living room.

The family wants to return from the beach barefoot. An entrance through the balcony allows them to remove their sandals and spread out a towel before entering. On breezy days, the open doors allow fresh air to circulate and bring the table closer to the view.
