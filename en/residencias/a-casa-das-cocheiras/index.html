A detailed decision

Wide range of materials available

Upon arrival after dark, the family needs to identify the gate, activate the access control system, and locate the path to the door. As part of the project, the team would consult with the landscape architect to determine the optimal placement of low-level lighting. A test at dusk would allow them to verify that the path is clearly visible and that the light does not inappropriately illuminate the windows of the support building.

The starting point

How the family wants to live

The room opens onto a veranda overlooking the pasture. The eaves provide shade over the armchairs and allow a clear view of the play area.

The family wants to watch the horses being trained. The balcony connects the living room to the track. Those returning from horseback riding can store their boots before entering, and the separate entrance to the stable workers' house preserves their privacy.
