Between the Living Room and the Garden

Explore the design decision

The bank follows the transition from the house to the garden.

A detailed decision

Wide range of materials available

Before ordering the stone for the bench, the team would compare two samples side-by-side on the veranda. The family could feel the texture, see the color next to the wood, and approve the choice, taking into account the price difference and the lead time.

The starting point

How the family wants to live

A scene includes a bench in the shade, a sliding door, and the transition from the living room to the garden. It depicts the family's preferred space within the home.

The starting point for this study is to imagine a family that enjoys having coffee on the veranda and likes to host guests at home. The transition between the living room and the garden should serve both of these functions, even when the sliding door is open.
