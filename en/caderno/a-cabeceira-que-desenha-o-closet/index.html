A headboard that shapes the dressing room

Illustrative image

The bed would face the window. The problem was the wardrobe, which also needed a wall. The idea of removing the bed from the room solved both issues. A half-wall of brick would create a backrest on one side and, on the other, provide space for dressing.

This headboard occupies part of the floor. Before the work begins, marking its outline and the outline of the mattress helps to visualize the suite as it would be on a typical morning. Is it possible to walk from one end of the bed while someone opens a drawer in the closet? When sitting, can the resident still see the tree from the window? Once the position is determined, the team can coordinate the electrical points and the finishing of the back wall, which will be visible to the person getting dressed.

The narrow countertop is used to place the glasses at the end of the night. In the morning, it's simply a matter of walking around the same wall to choose clothing. The bedside table has found a place in both routines.

In "The Courtyard House" the tree can be seen from the sofa. If the headboard divides the bedroom and the walk-in closet, Piguê would ensure that the height of the divider is determined from the pillow to preserve the view that motivated the bed's position.
