Lipstick in the window light

Illustrative image

The launch of La Beauté Louis Vuitton featured 55 shades of lipstick, curated by Pat McGrath. Konstantin Grcic designed the packaging. For those who have ever stood in a room with two red lipsticks in hand, trying to decide between them by the window, this range brings a familiar question: In what light do you typically choose the color you want to use?

A ideal location for makeup starts with the mirror, viewed from the seated position. Move closer to the bench, open your makeup bag, and observe your own face directly. A strong light source only in the ceiling can leave your eyes in shadow. It's easier to discuss positioning and intensity with the designer when you have a temporary mirror and your everyday makeup products readily available.

It's nice to have a place to rest your wrist while trying on a new red lipstick. And it's convenient to be able to keep the magnifying mirror in the first drawer, right next to the hand you use to apply it. These are small details that make a difference, whether you're discussing them with the carpenter or using them every morning.

Piguê considers the light from the window to be at eye level in "The Table by the Veranda" In the dressing area, she would perform the test while sitting with the resident, choosing the location for the mirror and checking where the hand reaches the first drawer.
