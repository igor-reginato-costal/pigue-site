A home in three shades of one color

Concept study from the Piguê collection.

In the blue room, the armchairs are illuminated from the front. The same color family creates depth in the folds of the curtain. A blue thread on the porcelain connects the two. A sense of connection is found even when the surfaces have different finishes.

To try it out at home, paint three movable panels. Place them near a wall in the morning and then near another wall in the evening. The choice becomes more personal when you can decide which one best complements your inherited rug or the light from the lamp you use for reading.

The color palette for "The Blue Room" was conceived by Piguê based on the relationship between the armchairs, rug, and porcelain. The next step would be to bring the painted tiles close to these pieces and observe them during use.
