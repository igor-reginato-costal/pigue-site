Locks and doors · Proposed retrospective edition · 21 May 2026

A handle for every door

Illustrative image

A view from the hallway, with a series of door handles at the same height, creates a sense of unity in the house. On the other side, each door represents a different routine: the front door welcomes visitors, the office requires silence during a conversation, and the powder room needs to offer privacy to a guest who has never been there before.

It is possible to maintain the lever design and vary its function. There are mechanical versions for different uses: standard, internal, and for bathrooms. In the bathroom, try the small latch that rotates internally. Can you understand its movement without further explanation? Also, ask the supplier to demonstrate the emergency release feature included in that version.

At the entrance, a discerning reader can enjoy classic fixtures in the bedrooms. Choose items that complement each other, such as the curve of a lever or the smooth sheen of a faucet. Then, allow each resident to try out the fixtures. The hand of someone with limited strength and the hand of someone carrying a child can reveal details that a beautiful hallway, no matter how well-designed, would never show on its own.

In "The Stable House" the paths of the family and the support house are independent. Piguê would take this difference into account when choosing the hardware, coordinating with the residents to determine which doors would receive keys, locks, or just privacy latches.
