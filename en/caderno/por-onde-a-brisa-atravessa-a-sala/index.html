The breeze through the living room

Illustrative image

In the first morning in the apartment, she opened the window next to the sofa. The curtains barely moved. It was only when she also opened the door to the balcony, on the other side of the living room, that she felt the breeze reach the coffee table. The difference was in the path the air could take.

When the outdoor air is clean, opening windows on opposite sides of the house can promote cross-ventilation. This exchange with the outside air helps to dilute the air inside, including airborne particles, especially during family gatherings. When planning the renovation, it's important to consider which windows can be opened simultaneously, safely and privately, while the living room is in use.

On hot days, the early morning can offer cooler air than the interior of the house. In the afternoon, if the outside temperature is higher than the temperature inside, closing the window and shading the glass can help to keep the heat out. The best time to open the window depends on the weather outside.

The open doors of "The Seaside Veranda" connect the living room to the sea breeze. In a different climate, Piguê would work with the architect to determine which openings the family could keep open simultaneously, while maintaining privacy.
