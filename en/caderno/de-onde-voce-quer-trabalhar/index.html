Where would you like to work?

Illustrative image

The best view in the apartment might very well belong to the desk. Someone who spends their mornings making difficult decisions might appreciate that street-facing window more than guests who come for dinner occasionally.

During a renovation, a morning spent working near the window reveals where the screen reflects and how far a conversation can be heard through the door. These observations provide the architect with a starting point. Before ordering the shelving unit, the construction team can consult with him about the door opening, the position of the outlets, and the location of the cables. The desk is then free for you to use for your work.

Some people need to set up two workstations side by side. Others need a partner to discuss financial matters privately. In both cases, it's useful to draw out the available space first. Then, you can decide where to place the items that were previously on the surface.

In the apartment "The Table by the Veranda" the city view is enjoyed by those seated at the table. If work is also being done there, Piguê would suggest opening the computer in the afternoon before deciding with the architect where the electrical outlet and curtains should be placed.
