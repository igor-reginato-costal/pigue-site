What the mirror sees

Concept study from the Piguê collection.

Before choosing a frame, sit or stand in the same spot where you usually get ready. What appears behind your shoulder? A small garden can double the size of the window. A lamp left in the same place can even make a very well-polished mirror uncomfortable.

At the 2026 Milan International Bathroom Exhibition, Salone highlighted the subtle integration of technology with materials. This concept translates well to the domestic environment: experiencing light directly on one's face and controlling the faucet with a wet hand. The choice of the sink, faucet height, and mirror placement should all be considered together.

The "The Courtyard House" design already guides the view from the living room. If the family also wants to bring it to the bathroom mirror, Piguê can test the reflection and privacy before the architect finalizes the window placement.
