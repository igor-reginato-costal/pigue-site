The bottle that waited for you

Illustrative image

Some bottles bring back the memory of the occasion. This one was from a trip with my sister. It was chosen for the first dinner at our new home. It's wonderful to find it when the day finally arrives, without having to move any of the other six bottles.

To design a private wine cellar, the existing collection is a great starting point. Some people organize by region, while others remember wines based on who gave them to them. Bottles of different shapes, unopened boxes, and a handful of labels that rotate weekly all require distinct spaces. If climate control is present, the team should anticipate where the equipment dissipates heat and how to access it for maintenance.

Near the table, there is often enough space for the corkscrew and the first glasses. The conversation about the trip begins there, with the bottle in the hands of the person who remembers exactly where it came from.

The porch of "The Stable House" is also a place for receiving guests. If wine is part of the family's lunches, Piguê would take the dimensions of the bottles to the wine cellar designer and study the route to the shaded table.
