The growth chart that moves with you

Illustrative image

The last thing you might see when emptying a room could be a series of marks on the doorframe. Next to one of them is a date and a note saying that someone stood on their toes. How do I send this part of the house to the following address?

A wooden measuring rail, designed to be mounted on the wall, provides a delicate solution to the problem. It can be used to measure the height of children for over a year. During installation, the team records the distance between the first mark and the floor, ensuring that the height remains consistent after the child grows. The carpenter can also incorporate a low shelf within easy reach of the child, and plan for a higher position for when the child is older.

In the new room, the first item might be a ruler that belonged to a cousin who came to stay for a while. The ruler already has a story attached to it, but it still has a good length of wood remaining.

The study "The Blue Room" gives way to a memory from another home. Regarding the measuring tape, Piguê would record the distance from the first mark to the floor and the method for removing the piece to keep the family up to date during the move.
