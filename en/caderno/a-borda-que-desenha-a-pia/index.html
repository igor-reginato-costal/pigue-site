Bathroom fixtures and fittings · Proposed retrospective edition · 5 Nov 2026

The edge that gives a basin its shape

Illustrative image

The act of washing one's face takes up more space than it appears. The elbows extend, the hands reach for the water, and the towel needs to be within easy reach. In the ensuite bathroom, this small ritual helps to choose the sink. In the guest bathroom, where visitors typically only wash their hands, a sink with a unique design can become the focal point due to the shape it creates on the countertop.

In the undermount sink, the sink body is visible. It's a good option to experience the height of the rim combined with the countertop. The inset sink is fixed underneath the countertop, leaving a clear space for pulling the water from the stone into the sink. The overmount sink has the rim resting on the cutout and surrounds the opening like a frame.

Another option is the semi-inset version, which extends beyond the front of the cabinet. This allows you to use a shallower cabinet in a narrow bathroom. Before ordering the countertop, mark this extension on the floor and open the door. If the passage remains comfortable and the towel is within reach, the design has already passed a good test.

In "The Courtyard House" Piguê proposes to demonstrate the transition from the room to the patio in full scale. In the bathroom, the test would involve marking the outline of the sink and opening the door, before the marble workers cut the countertop.
