Where a bouquet begins

Illustrative image

A branch brought in from the garden came through the large door, but the vase it deserves doesn't fit under the sink. Anyone who enjoys arranging flowers knows this little comedy. The house usually has space to display the bouquet. The pleasure begins a little earlier, in the corner where the bunches are opened and the selection is made.

In a renovation, the tallest pot in the collection could be used for the initial discussion about the sink. With it on the countertop, it's easy to see how far the faucet needs to extend and how much space the handles take up when lying flat. A drawer can be used for the scissors and the string. The pots used most frequently are placed at a height where they can be easily reached with both hands.

Then come the choices that no project anticipates. Today, the tallest stems are going to the hallway. A flower that broke near the head ends up in a small vase, alone, next to the bed.

The "Between the Living Room and the Garden" balcony raises a question that Piguê would ask to the person who picks flowers. Between the garden and the vase, where can you place the wet branches without having to cross the living room?
