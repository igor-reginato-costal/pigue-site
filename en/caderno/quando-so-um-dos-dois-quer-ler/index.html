Places to read and rest · Proposed retrospective edition · 13 Oct 2026

When only one of you wants to read

Illustrative image

Only a few pages remain. Beside her, someone has already fallen asleep. The reader holds the book a little higher to catch the light from the lamp, until her arm gets tired and she closes the novel at the best part. The next night, the story of the arm begins again.

The height of a reading lamp can be determined by using the everyday pillow. It varies significantly depending on whether someone reads almost sitting up or prefers to lie down. During a renovation, it's helpful to try out a movable lamp before installing it to see if the hand casts a shadow on the edge of the page and if the lamp appears in the eyes of the person on the other side. The switch should also be tested in this way, this time with the book closed and the head resting on the pillow.

The agreement is subtle. Anyone reading can turn the page. Someone who briefly wakes up will see the page illuminated, turn to the other side, and then go back to sleep.

The study "The Curved Duplex" distinguishes between light for reading and light for receiving. In the bedroom, Piguê would place the book on one side of the bed and the pillow of the person who had already slept on the other side.
