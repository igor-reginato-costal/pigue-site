Storage and organization · Proposed retrospective edition · 14 Apr 2026

Just the sheet and its pillowcase

Illustrative image

The guests arrive after lunch. The bed is set up with the sheets, but only one of the pillowcases is present. The other one is in the pile of spare linens, next to two nearly identical white pillowcases. Anyone who has experienced this knows how much time it can take to find a single pillowcase that fits perfectly.

In a bedroom with beds of different sizes, it may be easier to store each complete set separately. A strip of fabric can be used to keep sheets and pillowcases together. The family chooses how to identify each set, either by placing it on a shelf, using a small label, or by the color of the border. The useful measurement for carpentry is the folded size, as determined by the person in charge of the linens. Above this, the fingers need to reach in to retrieve the entire set.

Sometimes, the best organization is found on the other side of the door. In the bedroom, simply loosening the tie allows you to find everything you need. There's enough time to open the window before the doorbell rings.

In the study "The Table by the Veranda" the furniture is designed with the user's needs in mind. To create a wardrobe for an apartment, Piguê would provide the carpentry workshop with a set of bedding that had already been folded by the family, with the pillowcase and sheets kept together.
