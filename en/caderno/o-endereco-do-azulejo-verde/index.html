Where to find the green tile

Illustrative image

One year after the renovation, a part needs to be replaced near the tank. Does anyone remember having saved green tiles? There are three boxes in the storage room, and all three appear to contain the exact same shade of green until the tiles are placed on the wall.

During the handover of the project, the architect and the team can work with the family to determine which replacement materials to keep and in what quantity. The box should include the intended use, the manufacturer, the reference number, and the batch number, if available. A photograph of the original label, kept in the house's notebook, preserves what dust and time can erase. The heaviest boxes should be placed at the bottom, in a suitable location for the material, with the labels facing forward.

On the day of the exchange, the person responsible for the repair will find the box and inspect the part in the bathroom before starting the work. The resident only needs to remember one thing: the tile for the bathtub is on the bottom shelf.

The tile chosen for the "Between the Living Room and the Garden" bench can be identified by the approved sample. Piguê would take the same care with the tile, keeping a record in the house's notebook of the batch number and the address of the replacement box.
