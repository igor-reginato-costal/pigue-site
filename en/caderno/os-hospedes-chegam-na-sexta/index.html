Locks and doors · Proposed retrospective edition · 24 Jul 2026

The guests arrive on Friday

Illustrative image

The friends arrive at the country house on Friday. You can only leave the city on Saturday. With a traditional lock, the initial part of the welcome involves coordinating who will deliver the key and how they will return it afterward. If the times align, there's even time to show them where the coffee and cups are.

If check-in times rarely coincide, a digital lock with temporary access can combine the stay period. It's important to distinguish between two functions. A single-use password is for a single entry. A password with a start and end date can track the comings and goings of the weekend, in models that offer this option. This allows the family's usual code to remain reserved for residents.

Imagine guests leaving for dinner immediately on the first night. They need to be able to return safely. Check the chosen option, send the entry instructions privately, and provide a contact phone number. If you prefer to manage everything remotely, check beforehand which features of the model require a connection or an accessory. After that, the most important message might be to remind them to bring their own coffee.

The evening arrival is already a standard part of "The Stable House" experience. If the family is hosting guests before arriving at the property, Piguê can rehearse the arrival and departure for dinner, using the designated access route for guests.
