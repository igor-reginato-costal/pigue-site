A day to leave the window closed

Illustrative image

Typically, the first thing she does when arriving at the country house is to open the windows. That afternoon, there is a smoke alert in the area. The same action that usually brings in the scent of the garden can also draw smoke particles from the fires inside.

While the concentration of particles outdoors is high, the Ministry of Health advises keeping doors and windows closed to reduce the entry of smoke. Inside the home, it is advisable to postpone the use of fireplaces and candles. An air purifier suitable for the size of the room can help to reduce airborne particles. The filter in a standard air conditioning unit has a different function, and simply having a cool room does not indicate whether the air is clean.

To reopen windows, follow local air quality alerts. If closing the house causes the temperature to rise too much, seek a cooler and cleaner environment. On that day, both conditions are important.

The breeze is welcome in the "The Seaside Veranda" project. Piguê would also bring the issue of needing to close the doors due to smoke to the project, discussing with the designers how to keep the room shaded and comfortable.
