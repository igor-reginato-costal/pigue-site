Coming home with your hands full

Illustrative image

As I returned from the market, the basil was spilling out of the bag, and the key was at the bottom of my purse. This is a good time to appreciate the convenience of biometrics. A single fingerprint can unlock the door. For those who are only taking the essentials, a password could be even more useful than any command sent from a mobile phone.

However, a traditional mechanical lock requires a physical key. This is a simple option for those who appreciate this gesture and prefer to avoid registration and battery replacements. With digital locks, it's important to find out how the device alerts you when the battery is low and what alternative entry methods the manufacturer offers. This information is especially important for those who use biometric access almost exclusively.

In the store, perform the entire movement with your bag on your shoulder. Reach for the reader, grip the handle, and imagine the weight of your door. Also, check if the model is suitable for the thickness of the paper and for the exposure to rain in that location. The best indicator is what happens between the first touch and the moment you place your purchases down.

In The Stable House, arriving after dark helped shape the entrance. Piguê would test the lock as part of that same arrival, from the hand reaching for the reader to the light you need to find your key.
