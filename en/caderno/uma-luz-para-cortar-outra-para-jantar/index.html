The color of light · Proposed retrospective edition · 17 Sep 2026

One light for chopping, another for dinner

Illustrative image

The sauce is simmering on the stove, the tomatoes have already been washed, and the recipe specifies that they should be chopped very finely. The kitchen even seems bright. However, as soon as you approach the countertop, your own shoulder casts a shadow on it. Before replacing the light bulb with a whiter one, take note of where the light is coming from.

A installation of light under the cabinet can illuminate your hands while you cook. If you prefer a more neutral white, try something around 4000 K in this area. On the packaging, the lumens indicate the amount of light the bulb emits. The position of the light fixture is what helps direct that light towards the cutting board.

With the kitchen open to the living room, it's a good idea to have separate switches for the countertop and dining table. While someone is preparing the sauce, the person at the table can enjoy the warmer light from the pendant fixture. Also, test the light while seated. If the light strip under the cabinet appears as a bright line at eye level, ask someone to adjust its position before closing the cabinet.

The "The Table by the Veranda" allows for continued conversation while someone is passing through the room. In the open-plan kitchen, Piguê would coordinate two light settings: one for the person finishing preparing the sauce to see the cutting board, and another for those who have already sat down to enjoy dinner.
