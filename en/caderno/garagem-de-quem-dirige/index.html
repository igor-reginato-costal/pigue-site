A garage for someone who loves to drive

Concept study from the Piguê collection.

Some people return from a quick trip and decide to stay a little longer in the garage. The engine cools down, the door remains open, and a chair finds a place near the workbench. For this person, the process of designing the garage begins where the maneuvering ends.

When drawing the car, include its dimensions with the door open. Mark the path of someone carrying a bag and the location where helmets and gloves are stored. Low lighting can highlight the body of the car at night; a work surface requires additional lighting to distinguish similar washers.

The "The Stable House" area contains a classic car and a workbench. During the construction, Piguê can check that the door for the driver opens smoothly and that the workbench receives adequate light even with the car parked there.
