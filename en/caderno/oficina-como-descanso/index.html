Finding rest in the workshop

Concept study from the Piguê collection.

A whole afternoon can pass in the repair of a hinge. For someone who enjoys working with their hands, leaving a piece on the bench, taken apart until next Sunday, is part of the pleasure.

A workshop design might begin with the movements you make there. A shallow drawer for small parts, room to clamp a vice, a lamp that lights the work without casting your own arm’s shadow across it. Keep your most-used tools in sight. The arrangement of a bench is as personal as handwriting.

In the study "The Stable House" Piguê provides the workbench in the garage with its own dedicated space near the car. The type of tools the family wants to leave set up would guide the carpenter in determining the workbench's edge, fixing method, and lighting position.
