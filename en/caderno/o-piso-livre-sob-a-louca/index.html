Bathroom fixtures and fittings · Proposed retrospective edition · 25 Aug 2026

Clear floor beneath the bathroom fixtures

Illustrative image

In a small bathroom, the floor tiles can be attractive enough to warrant an extra few centimeters of visible surface. The wall-mounted toilet allows the tile to extend underneath the sink. This also makes it easier to reach a section that is often hidden.

The decision should be made before the installation begins. A suspended basin requires a support that is compatible with the wall and an outlet for the drain in the desired location. If the basin has a built-in trap, request to see the drawing to determine how the mechanism can be accessed for maintenance. When renovating, finding out how the existing installation was done can help you decide whether the change is worth the cost.

The floor-mounted option with an integrated box offers another possibility. The box is visible, immediately behind the seat. Compare the total depth of both solutions, including the wall that may need to be reinforced to accommodate the installation. Sometimes, the most valuable centimeters are those that remain in front of the knees.

In "The Courtyard House" the floor detail is addressed before the foundation is poured. When selecting a suspended basin, Piguê would consult with the designers to determine the final wall thickness, support structure, and access for maintenance.
