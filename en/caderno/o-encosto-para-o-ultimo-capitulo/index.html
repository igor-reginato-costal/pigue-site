Headboards · Proposed retrospective edition · 9 Nov 2026

A backrest for the last chapter

Illustrative image

In the store, she would choose the fabric based on its color. In the bedroom, she would ultimately choose based on the shoulder area. This is where the body meets the headboard when the pillow begins to compress, and there are still a few pages left to go before the bed is complete. A padded headboard serves primarily to provide comfort and support at this specific point.

To speak with the upholsterer, it's best to bring the measurements of the mattress and the pillow that comes with it. Sitting in the correct position, the resident can determine how much support they would like and whether a high seam or a button would be positioned just behind their neck. A large sample placed on the bed helps to choose the fabric and color in the light of the lamp. If the preference is for a removable cover, the design should allow for it to be removed after installation.

At night, the plum color deepens and almost blends into the shadows. What remains noticeable, even with closed eyes, is the soft edge where it usually rests against the arm.

The "The Curved Duplex" cherry-colored sofa changes appearance when the room lights dim. To choose a darker headboard, Piguê would suggest taking the upholstered sample to the bedside lamp and placing the reading pillow on it.
