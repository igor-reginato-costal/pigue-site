A home gym you look forward to using

Concept study from the Piguê collection.

The first repetition begins before you pick up a weight. You need to reach the space, open the window, find the water and feel like staying. In a home gym, which way the bench faces can matter as much as the equipment list.

Try your favorite exercise in the spot set aside for it. Look at what the mirror reflects, how far you can move, and where you’ll stretch afterwards. If the gym shares a wall with a bedroom, talk to the designers about equipment noise and when you’ll use it.

In The Stable House, the exercise pavilion faces the water. Before the glazing is ordered, Piguê would look at where the bench sits and where the person exercising stands, so the family can decide on the view and the privacy they want.
