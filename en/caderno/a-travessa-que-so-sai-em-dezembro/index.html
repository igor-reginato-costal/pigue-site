The serving dish that comes out in December

Illustrative image

The blue platter only appears at the Christmas table. Everyone remembers the fish that it holds. No one remembers how many plates need to be lifted to take it out of the cupboard. At the last Christmas dinner, it took two people. One held the platter while the other pulled it out using the only edge that was visible.

In the renovation of the kitchen, the piece itself can be directly integrated with the carpentry work. Standing upright in a single opening with a base and side supports that provide good stability, the edge is visible. The depth must allow the door to close properly, and the opening must be large enough for both hands to reach in and remove dishes. Before producing all the dividing panels, it is advisable to test the fit with the widest piece.

Nearby, there is the towel that the family usually uses that night. In the following December, the first question might already be about the fish.

The collection inherited from "The Blue Room" places the family's history at the center of the conversation with Piguê. In the display case, the first item to be featured could be the old serving dish, which is only available in December.
