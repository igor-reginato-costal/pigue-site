Cool through the night

Illustrative image

On the first warm night, the room temperature seemed comfortable near the door. Lying down, the resident noticed that the stream of air was directed directly at the pillow. The discomfort began when the device started operating and ended when the direction of the airflow was changed. Lowering the temperature further would have resolved another issue.

A typical residential split system cools and recirculates the indoor air. The system needs to be designed to allow for fresh air intake, either through openings at appropriate times or via a system that brings in outdoor air. During a renovation, the position of the bed, the amount of sunlight, and the intended use of the room help the designer determine the appropriate size and location for the equipment. It is also important to provide access for cleaning the filters and performing the maintenance recommended by the manufacturer.

During delivery, try the device in the position you typically sleep in. Test the speed, the direction of the fins, and the sound in the quiet of your bedroom. A comfortable fit is one that allows you to forget about the control on the bedside table.

Access to the light source is an integral part of the design of the "The Curved Duplex" ceiling. To climatize a bedroom, Piguê would bring together the architect and the installer before finalizing the ceiling, ensuring access to the appliance and directing the airflow over the bed.
