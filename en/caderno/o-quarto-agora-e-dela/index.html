The bedroom is hers now

Illustrative image

For years, the etching supplies came from a box and took up space on the dining table until it was put away. After her daughter moved out and took her bed, the mother entered the old room with the supplies under her arm. In the morning, the light came from the side. That was exactly where she wanted to work.

In the first week, all she needed were two sawhorses and a borrowed piece of wood. It was through trial and error that she discovered exactly where she needed to position the roller, how much space the newly printed sheets required, and where she could step back to view an entire proof. When it was time to order the carpentry work, she already knew to request shallow drawers for the paper and an open wall for hanging the finished pieces.

On the following visit, the daughter recognized the leaves of the tree in the first drawing. She had been waking up to that view for years. Now, she took a copy of the drawing home with her.

The apartment renovation in "The Table by the Veranda" begins with the homeowner's needs. For someone who is converting a bedroom into a studio, Piguê would focus on the size of the printed artwork and the distance at which the homeowner prefers to view it.
