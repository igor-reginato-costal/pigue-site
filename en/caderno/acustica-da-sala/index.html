Where to sit for a whole record

Concept study from the Piguê collection.

The piano should start quietly. If all you hear from the armchair is the air conditioner, it's worth trying out the room in silence before choosing any audio equipment. The pleasure of listening also depends on the existing background noise.

Bring a familiar piece of music into the conversation about the room. Tell the designer where you prefer to sit, whether you listen to music at night, and who sleeps in the adjacent room. This information will guide the decisions regarding the layout, door sealing, and furniture. Then comes the most personal choice: which record will you play to "open" the house?

The fireplace and the carpentry create the living area of "Between the Living Room and the Garden" If there were a comfortable armchair for listening to records, Piguê would gather the designers to test the device with the air conditioner running, at a normal volume.
