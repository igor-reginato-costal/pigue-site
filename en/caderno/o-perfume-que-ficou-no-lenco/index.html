Fragrance and memory · Proposed retrospective edition · 9 Nov 2025

The scent left on a scarf

Illustrative image

Sometimes, a handkerchief kept for years can bring back a person's scent even before the drawer is fully opened. The fragrance industry is well aware of this connection between matter and memory. In 2025, Guerlain celebrated the centenary of Shalimar, created by Jacques Guerlain in 1925, with an interpretation that returns to the vanilla notes of the original composition.

At home, smells also have a location. The scent of bergamot reminds me of the kitchen table. The verbena soap is associated with vacations. A friend arrives for dinner, and her perfume announces her presence just before she enters the room. Part of the pleasure comes from recognizing each person individually.

Therefore, when considering the scent of a room, it's important to think about where it ends. A fragrant flower could be placed in the entryway, leaving the dining table free for the aroma of freshly baked bread. And the handkerchief could be returned to its drawer, preserving a personal memory for another day.

In "The Seaside Veranda" opening the balcony allows the sea breeze to enter the living room. For those who enjoy a home with a pleasant scent, Piguê would discuss with her family about where to store the tissues and how to air out this corner when she wants to simply enjoy the fragrance of the garden.
