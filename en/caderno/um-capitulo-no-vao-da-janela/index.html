Places to read and rest · Proposed retrospective edition · 12 Aug 2026

A chapter in the window recess

Illustrative image

On a rainy Saturday, the best spot in the apartment might be the space in front of a window. Sitting next to the glass, with your feet tucked in, you can follow an entire conversation, and between pages, watch the umbrellas changing direction outside.

To design this bench, the first step is to consider the needs of the person who will be using it. Some people prefer to sit with their feet firmly on the ground, while others prefer to stretch their legs and rest their back against the side of the opening. A temporary base and the existing cushion help determine the seat depth. Once everything is in place, the team can also check if the window opens freely and if the curtain moves smoothly without getting caught on the cushion.

At the end of the bench, there might be a small piece of wood left over, enough for the mug. When the rain gets heavy, the coffee is there, and so is the next chapter.

The "The Courtyard House" study is viewed from the low sofa. If the family wants to read on the window sill, Piguê can test the view from the height of a temporary bench, already with the cushion that will provide back support.
