From the sauna to the garden

Concept study from the Piguê collection.

After the heat, the air in the garden feels cooler. The transition from opening the door to finding a bench in the shade is part of the pleasure of having a spa at home. A secluded view allows you to extend this moment even when there are visitors on the patio.

Visualize the path with your bare feet in mind. Where is the towel rack? Is there a support when getting out of the shower? When it gets dark, a low-level light indicates the return without disrupting the view of the garden. The stone, windows, and lighting fixtures all have a specific purpose for their placement.

The glass-enclosed pavilion of "The Stable House" brings together the gym and sauna overlooking the pool. Piguê would suggest walking from the sauna to the pool area at dusk to decide where to place the towel rack and the initial lighting for the path.
