Places to read and rest · Proposed retrospective edition · 12 Nov 2026

A veranda measured in swings of a hammock

Illustrative image

After lunch, she takes the book under her arm. She lies in the hammock, finds the page she was reading, and listens to the conversation at the table. She enjoys being close enough to laugh when someone tells a story. All she needs is for no one to have to ask permission to turn the page.

At the country house, it's advisable to bring your own hammock when the porch is still under construction. The open fabric occupies a different area than what you might expect, given the two attachment points. The architect can allocate space for the swing and for access to the garden. The team then checks for the necessary supports. Finally, you need to decide which side will have the bench where you can place your glasses and your book.

Sometimes, after three pages, the book returns to the table. The conversation continues for a while longer. Someone at the table notices and decides to postpone their coffee.

The shaded area, "Between the Living Room and the Garden" already invites a moment of relaxation. If the family prefers a swing, Piguê would take the dimensions to the architect so that the swing preserves the passage between the living room and the garden.
