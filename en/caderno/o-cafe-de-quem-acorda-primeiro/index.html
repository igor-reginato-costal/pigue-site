Coffee for the first one awake

Illustrative image

The first person to wake up hears the precise sounds of their own kitchen. The spoon in the drawer. The cupboard door that requires a second attempt to open. The coffee grinder that seems to awaken the entire apartment. A well-thought-out morning routine begins with this brief inventory of sounds.

For those who enjoy preparing their own coffee, it's a pleasure to have the necessary items readily available. A filter, a scale, and a mug can be stored together in a drawer under the counter, with space above for heating a teapot and resting a spoon. If two people drink coffee at different times, it's worth investigating how far the sound of the brewing process can be heard with the bedroom door closed.

The only remaining decision is where to place the first cup. It could be the stone bench that gets sunlight before the room, or the edge of the table from which one can see the street opening its doors. This detail should be considered as early as the choice of the machine.

The coffee on the balcony is the starting point in "Between the Living Room and the Garden" Piguê would ask who wakes up first to find out where they grind the beans and which rooms they pass through on their way to the garden. The answer helps in choosing the location for the countertop.
