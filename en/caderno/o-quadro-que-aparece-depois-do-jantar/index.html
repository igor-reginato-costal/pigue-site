The painting you notice after dinner

Illustrative image

The painting was there during lunch, but no one noticed it until the evening. The light from the window was replaced by the light from the lamps. Suddenly, one could see the brushstroke on the shoulder of the portrait. However, the same lamp was reflected in the glass of the frame on the armchair next to it.

During the renovation, the position of the artwork can be decided before the ceiling is finished. With the frame's dimensions marked on the wall, the architect and the lighting team can experiment with the lighting from the sofa. The family chooses their preferred view. During installation, the exact location and controls are already in place.

When setting the commands, think about who will be the last one to leave. It should be easy to turn off the dining room lights and leave only the necessary lights on for reading or walking through the room. The conversation will naturally shift when one person leaves.

The portrait between the two chandeliers provides a sense of scale to "The Blue Room" Piguê suggests marking the outline of the artwork on the wall and testing the lighting from the armchair before the electrician installs the fixtures.
