The first dinner after moving in

Concept study from the Piguê collection.

There are still some boxes in the hallway when someone suggests opening the wine. The friends bring bread, and the first meal takes place with mismatched dishes. It's a great opportunity to see if it's possible to dim the lights in the room without getting up from the table.

Before the move, walking through the apartment with the team allows them to practice these small details. Opening the balcony door, turning on just one light fixture, testing the faucet with a bowl in the sink. Having the light fixture, finish, and supplier references readily available later on saves the family time and reduces stress.

In "The Table by the Veranda" the table serves as a gathering space for friends and also provides clear passage to the balcony. Before handing over an apartment like this, Piguê could walk the family through the sequence of the first dinner, from the arrival with their bags to the final turning off of the lights.
