A growing family · Proposed retrospective edition · 18 Aug 2026

The older sister’s first gift

Illustrative image

In the drawing, there are four people. The youngest is almost the same size as her father, because she was the first to fit on the paper. The girl has chosen red for the frame and already knows where she wants to hang it in her brother's room. From the hallway, she wants to be able to show the gift to anyone who comes in.

Waiting for a baby brings many adult decisions into the home. There are also choices that the future sister can make. The father is responsible for setting up the game. She chooses the background color, decides who is who, and decides whether or not to sign. The game board from yesterday remains set up in her room. Not every available space needs to have a new function.

One day, the brother will ask why the portrait is so large. It's possible that both of them have already forgotten the color of the wall. But the right person to answer will be right there, standing next to them.

The inherited portrait occupies the center of "The Blue Room" In the nursery, the important artwork for the family could be the first drawing made by the sister. Piguê could foresee a place where she would be able to see it from her height.
