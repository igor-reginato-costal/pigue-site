Funding for construction

How much should you set aside to start the construction? The answer depends on the land, the budget for the house, and when the funds need to be available. These are the key factors to consider when discussing construction loan options with Piguê.

Do you already own the land?

If the land is yours, you can explore financing options for the construction. If you are still in the process of choosing where to live, there are institutions that allow you to combine the purchase of the land and the construction into a single loan. The available option depends on the analysis of the family's needs, the land, and the project by the financial institution.

When are funds released for construction?

In some cases, the institution releases the funds in stages, as construction progress is verified. Before finalizing the contract, compare the planned disbursement dates with the dates for purchasing materials and paying the team. This will allow you to estimate how much you will need available before the next payment.

What to compare in the proposals?

Ask for illustrations based on the same loan amount and term. Check how much you need to contribute yourself, which payments fall due during construction, and what your installments will be after handover. Compare the Custo Efetivo Total (CET), the Brazilian measure of the total cost of borrowing, including interest, fees, taxes and other credit charges. Each lender sets its own terms and decides whether to approve the loan.

Engineering and BIM Technology

Seeing the whole house before building begins

When you spend years building or renovating your home, it's important to be able to make decisions calmly. Before making purchases and starting work, the team has the most flexibility to adjust the project.

In BIM projects, BIM stands for Building Information Modeling, which involves creating models for architecture, structure, and systems. This allows designers to visualize where systems compete for space and to explore alternative solutions while the design is still in its initial stages.

Engineering expertise guides the decision-making process. The team assesses the property's conditions, discusses alternatives with the architect, and explains to the family the changes in the outcome, cost, and timeline. This coordination helps to minimize rework and last-minute decisions on the construction site.

Questions about working with us

What kinds of projects does Piguê manage?

We coordinate new homes and renovations of houses and apartments. For a new build, we bring the architectural, structural and building services designs together before planning the work on site. For a renovation, we begin with the property as it stands and what your family hopes to change or preserve. See where we work.

Can we talk to Piguê before buying a property?

Yes. We can meet while you’re considering a property to discuss the changes you have in mind and the support you may need.

How do you plan renovations in an apartment building?

We start with the building rules and the documents the management team requires. With the responsible professionals, we plan the work, permitted hours, protection for shared spaces, and how materials will travel in the lift. Your family’s schedule accounts for those constraints.

How do we choose an architect?

If you already have an architect, we coordinate the work with their practice. If you’d like an introduction, we ask about your references, routines and preferences so we can introduce practices whose work feels right for your family. The choice is yours.

How do payments work?

Piguê charges a management fee for the agreed scope. Your family pays suppliers and contractors directly. We bring any proposed extension of scope to you for approval.

Who can we turn to after we move in?

Your project manager remains your point of contact for settling-in adjustments covered by the proposal. Your home manual brings warranties and useful contacts together.

How do you approach daylight and ventilation?

We begin with when you use each room. A bedroom might need morning light to wake up to and shade for an afternoon rest. With your architect, we look at window orientation, shading and how air can move through the space. In a renovation, we start with the existing windows and the changes the building allows.

How does the garden shape the construction details?

Where a living room opens onto a courtyard, the threshold depends on the indoor floor level, the slope outside and where the water drains. Before the stone is laid, the site team checks those levels with the architect and landscape designer. That way, the threshold works as well for the people using the room as for the person watering the garden.

I have a plot and an architect in Alphaville. Who coordinates construction?

Your architect remains the author of the design. Piguê brings the designers together, organizes estimates and follows the work on site. Before approving a change, your family sees how it affects cost and timing. Tell us about your project in Alphaville.

I’m considering a house in Granja Viana. When should we discuss the renovation?

We can start before you buy. Tell us what you hope to keep, what you’d like to change and when you’d like to move in. Together we can decide what support makes sense and which questions call for a technical assessment. Talk to us about the property in Granja Viana.

Can I renovate my apartment in Jardins with the architect I’ve chosen?

Piguê coordinates construction with your architect’s practice. With the responsible professionals, we organize the documents your building requires. The schedule accounts for permitted hours, lift access and protection for shared areas. Your family stays involved in procurement and construction decisions.
