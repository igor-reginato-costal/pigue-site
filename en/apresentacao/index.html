Company presentation

What would you like to keep in your home?

We coordinate home construction and renovation alongside the family and their architect.

A living room open to the terrace. The same wooden floor.

Imagine a family who want more room around the table and hope to keep their apartment’s wooden floor. Opening up the living room raises a question. How can the floor meet the terrace and still feel continuous?

Who takes care of what?

Your architect designs the solution. Piguê brings the designers, suppliers and site team together. You choose the finish and approve the investment.

What do you see before you decide?

In this example, the family compares a restoration sample with the existing floor in the light of their own living room.

Explore the decision

The drawing shows where the floors meet. The estimate itemises the restoration and the new section. The team confirms the installation date and the family signs off before the order is placed.

How do you follow up on what was agreed?

  1. Before ordering

    The project manager records the approved sample and agreed date.

  2. During construction

    The site team checks the installed finish against the sample.

  3. At handover

    At the walkthrough, the family checks the result and receives the outstanding items and support contacts.
