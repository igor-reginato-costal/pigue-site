Where do colors come from?

The sand and blue tones originate from the neutral, sandy and smoky blue hues of the Emporio Armani's Autumn/Winter 2025 Women's Fashion Show. For the plum, the reference is the depth of Chanel Rouge Noir 155 nail polish. Goiaba is a more gentle interpretation of Rouge Dior 999 Velvet lipstick.

To choose the paint, paint a sample on a piece of wood and fabric from the room and compare it to the existing colors. Observe the color in both daylight and under the light of the lamps.

Approach and technology

What engineering can resolve before construction begins

Imagine a room where the architect designed the ceiling to match the height of the windows. If an air conditioning duct crosses a beam, this alignment could be disrupted.

In BIM projects, the team brings together architectural, structural, and MEP (mechanical, electrical, and plumbing) elements into a single digital model. This allows for the identification of conflicts, the exploration of alternative designs with the architect, and the explanation of the impact on cost and schedule to the client before construction begins.

The Piguê approach

What distinguishes a finely built home?

Listen
Where do you like to read? This information helps in choosing the right window light, the ideal position for your armchair, and a convenient spot to keep your blanket nearby.
Accuracy
At the threshold between living room and terrace, stone meets the door track and the floor. Before the stone is laid, the team must resolve all three levels and where the water will drain.
Caution
During the handover, we will walk through the house with you, testing the lights and faucets. After the move, our team will assist with any necessary adjustments, and you will receive the warranty information in the house's documentation.
