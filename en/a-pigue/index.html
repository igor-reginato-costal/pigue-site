Residential construction and renovation

We coordinate new builds and renovations for houses and apartments, primarily in Greater São Paulo. For projects inland or on the coast of São Paulo state, we consider the location and scope individually. We begin by asking how you want to live. Alongside your architect, we bring the designers and the people on site together so that each choice is built as intended. From a timber sample to the fit of a door, we look after the details and stay by your family’s side beyond moving day.

What unites us

When someone builds a house, they share very personal decisions with others. They discuss their lifestyle, their financial plans, and their timeline for moving in. For us, hearing this from a family is a commitment to supporting them.

Humberto Schott works with families, understanding the reasons behind their decisions. Luiz Júnior fosters relationships and connects the professionals who can carry them out. Igor Reginato coordinates the operation to ensure that decisions are translated into action on the construction site.

Our society functions best when these responsibilities are clearly defined. When a change in the project is necessary, the person on the construction site knows the reason for the original request. And the person communicating with the family can explain the changes, the reasons behind them, and the remaining options.

Sustainability

A house that is worth keeping

Before a renovation, Piguê walks through the home with the family and their architect to decide what is worth keeping. A timber floor can be restored. A stone worktop can find a new place in the home.

On site, we plan cuts, protect finishes and set aside offcuts for reuse. When comparing materials, we consider how long they will last and, where data is available, the emissions associated with each option.

A tree that shades the living room and a window that catches the breeze make the home comfortable long after move-in. At handover, the home manual shows the family how to care for what they chose to keep.
